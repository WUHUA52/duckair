# DuckAir city backgrounds — viewpoints, references, simplifications

Each city is one real river flight line seen from a camera flying along the river and looking across at one bank. Landmarks are placed from map coordinates (position along the river, distance inland → parallax layer). The other bank appears only in the Front/Rear/3D views, which use the same layout.

Shared simplification: each route loops; at the loop seam a quiet stretch (trees, low blocks) joins the ends and distant layers fade into haze across it.

## Philadelphia

**Viewpoint:** Down the Schuylkill, heading south, looking east at Center City.

**Route:** Boathouse Row and Lemon Hill → Fairmount Dam → Art Museum above the Water Works → Spring Garden and Vine Street bridges → Cathedral dome, Three Logan, Comcast Center, Comcast Technology Center, BNY Mellon, Blue Cross, Commerce Square, Murano, PECO → JFK and Market Street bridges, City Hall framed down Market Street → One and Two Liberty Place → Chestnut and Walnut bridges, 2400 Chestnut, Schuylkill Banks boardwalk, Fitler Square → South Street Bridge. West bank (30th Street Station, Cira Centre, FMC Tower) only in Front/Rear/3D.

**Deliberate simplifications:**
- River straightened; depth compressed (telephoto-like).
- Buildings drawn at ~0.8 of true perspective size; City Hall ~1.25×.
- Ben Franklin Bridge removed (it is on the Delaware).
- 3D river wider than real.

## New York City

**Viewpoint:** North up the East River from the Battery to the Queensboro Bridge, looking west at Manhattan.

**Route:** Battery Maritime Building → 55 Water, 40 Wall, 70 Pine, One WTC behind → Seaport / Pier 17 → Woolworth, 8 Spruce, Municipal Building → Brooklyn Bridge → Manhattan Bridge, One Manhattan Square → Lower East Side → Williamsburg Bridge → Con Ed stacks, Stuyvesant Town, Waterside, Bellevue → American Copper → Empire State behind → Tudor City, UN, Chrysler, Trump World, Citigroup, 432 Park → Queensboro Bridge. Brooklyn/Queens only in 3D.

**Deliberate simplifications:**
- Lower East Side to Kips Bay compressed ~4× so Lower Manhattan and Midtown both fit the loop.
- One WTC, Empire State, Chrysler, Woolworth, Municipal Building, 432 Park and the bridges drawn 1.1–1.3×.
- Queensboro tower placed at the shoreline.

## Paris

**Viewpoint:** West (downstream) along the Seine, looking south at the Left Bank, from Île Saint-Louis to the Eiffel Tower.

**Route:** Île Saint-Louis → Notre-Dame (Panthéon behind) → Conciergerie and Sainte-Chapelle → Pont Neuf and the Vert-Galant → Monnaie, Institut de France with Pont des Arts → Pont Royal → Musée d'Orsay → Senghor footbridge → Palais Bourbon → Pont Alexandre III with Invalides on its esplanade → American Church → Pont de l'Alma → Quai Branly → Pont d'Iéna and the Eiffel Tower → Bir-Hakeim. Right Bank (Louvre, Concorde, Grand Palais, Chaillot) only in 3D.

**Deliberate simplifications:**
- Ordinary blocks ~1.3× (3 px per floor); Notre-Dame ~1.7×; distant domes and Montparnasse up to ~4×; Eiffel ~1.2×.
- Both Notre-Dame west towers shown, though from due north they overlap.
- Water between the islands not shown; Montparnasse depth reduced.

## Tokyo

**Viewpoint:** South (downstream) along the Sumida, looking east at Sumida and Kōtō wards. Tokyo Skytree is the tower of this view; Tokyo Tower is behind the camera (3D only).

**Route:** Sumida Park cherries → Kototoi Bridge → Tobu railway bridge with Skytree behind → red Azuma Bridge and the Asahi group (Beer Tower, golden flame) → Shuto Route 6 on the bank → Komagata, Umaya, Kuramae bridges → Kokugikan and Edo-Tokyo Museum → Sōbu line bridge → Ryōgoku Bridge → Shin-Ōhashi → blue Kiyosu Bridge → Eitai Bridge → Chūō-Ōhashi and the River City 21 towers. Asakusa/Sensō-ji and Tokyo Tower only in 3D.

**Deliberate simplifications:**
- Fabric drawn ~1.9× tall, 1.3× wide; Skytree ~1.45×, Asahi ~1.6×, Kokugikan ~1.9×.
- Skytree placed on the line of the Tobu tracks, as seen from Asakusa.
- Some bridge types simplified; head-on arches flatten.

## London

**Viewpoint:** East (downstream) along the Thames, looking north from Westminster to Tower Bridge. South Bank (London Eye, Shard, Tate Modern) is behind the camera (3D only).

**Route:** Victoria Tower Gardens → Palace of Westminster (Victoria Tower, Central Tower, Elizabeth Tower) → Westminster Bridge → MoD, Whitehall Court → Hungerford/Jubilee bridges, Charing Cross → Cleopatra's Needle, Shell Mex House → Waterloo Bridge → Somerset House → Temple → Blackfriars bridges → St Paul's on the Millennium Bridge axis → Southwark Bridge, Cannon Street → London Bridge, Monument → City cluster (Walkie-Talkie, Cheesegrater, 22 Bishopsgate, Gherkin, Scalpel) → Tower of London → Tower Bridge → St Katharine Docks.

**Deliberate simplifications:**
- River straightened (the Waterloo bend removed).
- East of Westminster, fabric ~1.6× tall; St Paul's ~1.65×, City towers ~1.65×, White Tower ~2×, Tower Bridge ~1.15×.
- Route squeezed 0.9× to leave a quiet stretch at the loop seam.
